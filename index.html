Official* Rules of the Court

1. No volleying in the kitchen

Also no cooking. The kitchen is a state of mind.

2. Call the score loudly

If nobody remembers it, the most confident voice wins.

3. Say "nice shot" generously

Even when it wasn't. Especially when it wasn't.

4. The paddle is not a fan

Yes, it's Florida. Yes, it's hot. Still no.

5. Hydrate or dill-iminate

Water breaks are mandatory. Pickle juice is optional.

6. Respect the Queen

If The Star is in the kitchen, kindly let her cook.

7. No apologizing to the ball

It knows what it did.

8. Celebrations limited to 3 seconds

Victory dances over 3 seconds require a permit.

*Not official. For real rules, ask a real referee.
